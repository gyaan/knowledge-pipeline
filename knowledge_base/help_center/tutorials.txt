CloudSupport Pro Tutorials

Step-by-step tutorials to help you master CloudSupport Pro features.

Tutorial 1: Setting Up Your First Support Channel

In this tutorial, you will configure email as your first support channel.

Step 1: Navigate to Settings > Channels > Email from your admin dashboard.
Step 2: Click "Add Email Channel" and enter your support email address (e.g., support@yourcompany.com).
Step 3: Choose your connection method. For Gmail, use OAuth authentication. For other providers, enter IMAP/SMTP settings.
Step 4: Configure routing rules. Choose which team or agent should receive tickets from this channel.
Step 5: Set up an auto-reply template that customers receive when their email is converted to a ticket.
Step 6: Send a test email to verify everything works correctly.
Step 7: Enable the channel when you are satisfied with the configuration.

Your email channel is now live. All incoming emails will automatically create support tickets.

Tutorial 2: Creating Your First Automation Rule

Automations save time by handling repetitive tasks automatically.

Step 1: Go to Settings > Automations > Create New Rule.
Step 2: Name your automation (e.g., "Auto-assign billing tickets").
Step 3: Define the trigger condition. Select "When a ticket is created" and add a condition: "Category equals Billing."
Step 4: Define the action. Select "Assign to team" and choose your billing support team.
Step 5: Optionally add additional actions like setting priority, adding tags, or sending notifications.
Step 6: Set the rule priority. Rules with lower numbers execute first.
Step 7: Click "Test Rule" to preview which existing tickets would match.
Step 8: Enable the automation.

The automation will now run on all new tickets matching your conditions.

Tutorial 3: Installing the Live Chat Widget

Add live chat to your website to provide real-time support.

Step 1: Go to Settings > Channels > Live Chat.
Step 2: Customize the widget appearance. Choose colors, position (bottom-right or bottom-left), and welcome message.
Step 3: Configure business hours. Set when live chat is available and what message to show outside hours.
Step 4: Copy the JavaScript snippet provided.
Step 5: Paste the snippet into your website's HTML, just before the closing body tag.
Step 6: Visit your website and verify the chat widget appears.
Step 7: Test by sending a message through the widget and confirming it appears in your CloudSupport Pro dashboard.

For single-page applications (React, Vue, Angular), you can also install the widget via npm package @cloudsupportpro/chat-widget.

Tutorial 4: Building a Knowledge Base

A knowledge base helps customers find answers without contacting support.

Step 1: Navigate to Knowledge Base > Articles from your dashboard.
Step 2: Create categories to organize your content (e.g., Getting Started, Billing, Troubleshooting).
Step 3: Click "New Article" and select a category.
Step 4: Write your article using the rich text editor. Include a clear title, introduction, step-by-step instructions, and related links.
Step 5: Add tags to improve search discoverability.
Step 6: Preview the article to check formatting.
Step 7: Publish the article. It will be immediately available in your customer-facing help center.
Step 8: Share the article URL in canned responses to efficiently answer common questions.

Tip: Start with articles for your top 10 most common support questions. This alone can reduce ticket volume by 20-30%.

Tutorial 5: Setting Up SLA Policies

SLA policies ensure timely responses and resolutions.

Step 1: Go to Settings > SLA Policies > Create New Policy.
Step 2: Name your policy (e.g., "Premium Customer SLA").
Step 3: Define conditions for when this policy applies. For example, customer plan equals "Enterprise" or priority equals "Critical."
Step 4: Set first response time targets (e.g., 1 hour for critical, 4 hours for high, 8 hours for medium).
Step 5: Set resolution time targets (e.g., 4 hours for critical, 24 hours for high, 72 hours for medium).
Step 6: Configure escalation actions for approaching and breached SLAs.
Step 7: Set business hours for SLA calculation (or choose 24/7 for critical SLAs).
Step 8: Save and enable the policy.

SLA timers will now appear on applicable tickets, helping agents prioritize their work.

Tutorial 6: Generating Reports

Use reports to understand your support performance.

Step 1: Navigate to Analytics > Reports from your dashboard.
Step 2: Select a report type: Ticket Volume, Response Times, Agent Performance, or Customer Satisfaction.
Step 3: Set the date range for your report.
Step 4: Apply filters to focus on specific teams, channels, or categories.
Step 5: Review the generated charts and tables.
Step 6: Click "Export" to download the report as CSV or PDF.
Step 7: Schedule recurring reports by clicking "Schedule" and selecting frequency and recipients.

Tip: Set up a weekly summary report delivered to your email every Monday morning for a quick overview of the previous week's support performance.

Tutorial 7: Configuring Agent Roles and Permissions

Control what each team member can see and do.

Step 1: Go to Settings > Team > Roles.
Step 2: Review the default roles (Owner, Admin, Agent, Viewer) and their permissions.
Step 3: To create a custom role, click "New Role" and name it (e.g., "Senior Agent").
Step 4: Select permissions for the role. Permissions are grouped by area: Tickets, Knowledge Base, Reporting, Settings, and Integrations.
Step 5: Save the custom role.
Step 6: Assign the role to team members in Settings > Team > Members by editing their profile and selecting the new role.

Custom roles allow you to give team leads additional permissions like managing canned responses or viewing reports without full admin access.
