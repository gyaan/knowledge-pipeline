Frequently Asked Questions - Account Management

Q: How do I create an account?
A: Creating an account is easy:
1. Go to cloudsupportpro.com
2. Click "Start Free Trial"
3. Enter your email and create a password
4. Verify your email address
5. Complete the setup wizard
6. Invite your team members

Q: How do I reset my password?
A: If you forgot your password:
1. Go to the login page
2. Click "Forgot Password?"
3. Enter your email address
4. Check your email for a reset link
5. Click the link and create a new password

The reset link is valid for 24 hours.

Q: Can I have multiple accounts?
A: Each email address can only be associated with one account. However, you can be an agent on multiple company accounts if invited by different organizations.

Q: How do I add team members?
A: To add agents to your account:
1. Go to Settings > Team
2. Click "Invite Agent"
3. Enter their email address
4. Select their role (Admin, Agent, or Viewer)
5. Choose which teams they should join
6. Click "Send Invitation"

They'll receive an email to create their account.

Q: What are the different user roles?
A: We have four user roles:

Owner - Full access to everything including billing
Admin - Manage agents, settings, and integrations (no billing access)
Agent - Handle tickets, access reports, manage own settings
Viewer - Read-only access to tickets and reports

Q: How do I cancel my account?
A: We're sorry to see you go! To cancel:
1. Log in as the account owner
2. Go to Settings > Billing
3. Click "Cancel Subscription"
4. Select a reason (optional)
5. Confirm cancellation
6. Export your data if needed

Your account remains active until the end of your billing period.

Q: Can I reactivate a canceled account?
A: Yes! If you canceled within the last 90 days:
1. Log in with your original credentials
2. Go to Settings > Billing
3. Click "Reactivate Account"
4. Select a plan and enter payment information

All your data will be restored.

Q: How do I change my account email?
A: Only the account owner can change the primary email:
1. Go to Settings > Account
2. Click "Change Email"
3. Enter your new email address
4. Verify the new email
5. Confirm the change

Q: What is two-factor authentication (2FA)?
A: 2FA adds an extra layer of security to your account. When enabled, you'll need both your password and a code from your phone to log in. We support:
- SMS codes
- Authenticator apps (Google Authenticator, Authy)
- Backup codes

Enable 2FA in Settings > Security.

Q: Can I transfer account ownership?
A: Yes! The current owner can transfer ownership:
1. Go to Settings > Team
2. Find the agent you want to make owner
3. Click the three-dot menu
4. Select "Transfer Ownership"
5. Confirm the transfer

The new owner must be an existing Admin on the account.

Q: How do I update my billing information?
A: To update payment methods:
1. Go to Settings > Billing
2. Click "Payment Methods"
3. Add a new card or update existing one
4. Set as default if desired
5. Remove old payment methods if needed

Q: What if my payment fails?
A: If a payment fails:
- We'll automatically retry 3 times over 10 days
- You'll receive email notifications
- Your account will remain active during this period
- Update your payment method to avoid service interruption
- After 10 days, your account will be suspended (data retained for 30 days)
